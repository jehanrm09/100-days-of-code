You are given a list of "Sensor Readings," and you must find which readings
are "Alerts" (values that exceed a certain threshold) and calculate their average
to see the severity of the issue.
The Problem:
 * You have a list: readings = [12, 45, 67, 23, 89, 34, 99, 10, 56]
 * The threshold is 60.
 * You must use a for loop to look at every number.
 * The Goal: Create a new list called alerts, add only the numbers above the threshold to it, and then calculate the average of those alerts.

--- EXPECTED OUTPUT ---
Analyzing 9 sensor readings...
ALERT: High value detected: 67
ALERT: High value detected: 89
ALERT: High value detected: 99
--- Analysis Complete ---
Total Alerts Found: 3
Average Alert Severity: 85.00
