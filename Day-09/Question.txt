
The Problem: You need to generate a perfect 5*5 multiplication table grid.

The LeetCode Twist: 
1. You cannot simply print lines of text. You must use two loops: an outer 
loop for rows and an inner loop for columns.
2. You must use "String Formatting" to ensure the numbers align perfectly in 
columns (so a "10" takes up the same space as a "2").3. This is the foundation for
understanding O(n^2) Time Complexity—where the amount of work the computer does grows
squared relative to the input.

Expected Terminal Output:-
--- 5x5 Multiplication Table ---
  1   2   3   4   5 
  2   4   6   8  10 
  3   6   9  12  15 
  4   8  12  16  20 
  5  10  15  20  25
