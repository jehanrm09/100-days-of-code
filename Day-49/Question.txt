

Day 49: The "ID Compressor" Challenge

The Problem (LeetCode "String Compression" style):
Given a string of characters (like an ID), compress it by replacing consecutive
repeating characters with the character followed by the count. If the "compressed"
string isn't actually shorter than the original, return the original.

The "Human" way to think about it:
Imagine you are reading a list of IDs to a friend over the phone. Instead of
saying "A, A, A, B, B, C," you would say "Three A's, Two B's, One C."
