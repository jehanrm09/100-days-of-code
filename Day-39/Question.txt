

The Day 39 Challenge: "The System Controller"
The Problem: Your registration portal needs a "Maximum Capacity" and a fixed 
list of "Industry Types." You want to prevent new registrations if the limit is reached.

The Logic:
1. Define your Global Constants at the very top of your script.
2. Use an if statement to check your database length against your MAX_LIMIT.
3. Use the constants to provide the user with a list of valid choices.
