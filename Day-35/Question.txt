

The Day 35 Challenge: "The Record Manager"
The Problem: You have multiple students, and you want to calculate how many 
students are from a specific college or simply list their names professionally.

The Logic:
1. Create a list to hold multiple dictionaries.
2. Add (Append) a few "Student Objects" to that list.
3. Use a loop to "Unpack" the dictionaries and print a formatted summary.
