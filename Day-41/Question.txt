

The Day 41 Challenge: "The Automatic Time-Stamper"
The Problem: Your "Company Registration" form saves data, but you 
don't know who registered first. You need to add a "Created At" 
field to every dictionary automatically.

The Logic:
1. Import the datetime module.
2. Capture the current time at the moment of registration.
3. Format it into a clean, human-readable string.
4. Store it inside your dictionary object.
