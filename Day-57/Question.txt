

Day 57: List Intersection

The Logic:
1. We have two lists: list_a and list_b.
2. To make it fast, we turn the first list into a Set. Searching in
   a set takes O(1) time, while searching in a list takes O(n) time.
3. We loop through the second list and check if each item exists in our set.
4. If it does, we add it to our results and remove it from the set.
