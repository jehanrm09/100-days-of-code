

The Day 37 Challenge: "The Skill Upgrader"
The Problem: A student has completed a new course and wants to 
update the "Skill" listed in their registration profile.

The Logic:
1. Ask the user for the Name of the student to update.
2. Search the list of dictionaries.
3. If found, ask for the New Skill.
4. Update the dictionary and show the "Before" vs. "After."
