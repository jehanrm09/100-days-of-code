

Day 47: The "Two Sum" Challenge

The Official Problem Statement:"Given an array of integers nums and an integer
 target, return indices of the two numbers such that they add up to target.

"The Constraints:
1. Time Complexity: You must solve this in O(n).
2. The Trap: If you use a nested loop (loop inside a loop), your complexity is 
   O(n^2), which is considered a "fail" in technical interviews for this problem.
3. The Solution: You must use a Hash Map (Dictionary) to remember numbers you have
   already seen.
