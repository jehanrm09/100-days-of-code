

The Day 33 Challenge: "The University Reporter"
The Problem: Your students.txt is a big, messy list. Your boss wants to 
see a report that groups students by their University so it's easier to read.

The Logic:
1. Define Categories: Create a list of the colleges you expect
   (e.g., ["Ganpat", "Mehsana", "Ahmedabad"]).
2. Outer Loop: Start with the first college.
3. Inner Loop: Check every student in your database. If their
   "College" field matches, print them under that heading.
4. Repeat: Move to the next college.
