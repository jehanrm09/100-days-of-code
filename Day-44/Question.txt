

Day 44: The "Unique Pair" Algorithm

The Scenario: In your registration system, you have a list of 
Company Names and a list of Available Slots. Some companies are 
duplicates or have slightly different names.

The LeetCode Challenge: Given an array of strings names, return 
the length of the longest "Unique Sequence" you can form without 
repeating a name, while ignoring case and extra spaces.
