

The Day 30 Challenge: "The Student Withdrawal System"
The Problem: A student has requested to be removed from the registration list.
You need to find them by name and remove their entry completely.

The Logic:
1. Load: Read students.txt into a list.
2. Target: Ask for the name of the student to remove.
3. The Filter: Use a List Comprehension (this is the "Pro" way) to create a new
   list that contains everyone unless their name matches the target.
4. Confirm: Check if the new list is smaller than the old one. If it is, someone
   was deleted!
5. Save: Overwrite the file.
