

The Day 40 Challenge: "The Analytics Engine"
The Goal: Create a function that scans your entire database 
(your list of dictionaries) and prints a high-level summary.

The Logic:
1. Counters: Initialize variables to count different categories.
2. Loop & Logic: Iterate through the database and update counters based on the dictionary values.
3. Calculations: Use your MAX_CAPACITY constant to figure out the "Fill Percentage."
