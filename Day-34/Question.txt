

The Day 34 Challenge: "The Object Mapper"
The Problem: You have a line from your file, but you want to turn it 
into a smart "Object" so you can easily use it in your code.

The Logic:
1. Split: Take a string from your file.
2. Map: Assign each part of that string to a Key in a dictionary.
3. Process: Use the keys to print a beautiful, custom message.
